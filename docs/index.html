Beyond the automated test suite, I registered two accounts, matched them through Quick Match, and played moves across two independent browser sessions — watching the board, clocks, and move list stay in sync in real time. That process caught two real defects, which are now fixed and reverified.

Found live
A false "opponent reconnected" toast fired on every game join, not just genuine reconnections.

Found live

Scope

What's built, and what's honestly not

Two items are intentionally partial rather than faked — see the notes below.

Chess engine & rules

  • ✓Full legal-move validation, enforced only on the server
  • ✓Castling both sides, en passant, all four promotions
  • ✓Check, checkmate, and stalemate detection
  • ✓Threefold repetition, fifty-move rule, insufficient material

Real-time multiplayer

  • ✓Socket.IO live play with instant sync
  • ✓Server-side clocks across 10 built-in time controls
  • ✓Disconnect/reconnect handling with a grace period
  • ✓Draw offers with cooldown, resignation, timeouts

Accounts & security

  • ✓Register, login, forgot/reset password
  • ✓bcrypt hashing, httpOnly JWT sessions
  • ✓CSRF protection, rate limiting, security headers
  • ✓Every mutating endpoint validated server-side

Matchmaking & ratings

  • ✓Rating-based Quick Match, widens the longer you wait
  • ✓Friend challenges & shareable invite links
  • ✓Separate Elo rating per time control
  • ✓Global, country, and friends leaderboards

Social & history

  • ✓Friends: search, request, accept, presence
  • ✓In-game chat with quick messages and mute
  • ✓Full game history and move-by-move replay
  • iReplay shows a real material-balance bar, not fabricated engine analysis

Admin, anti-cheat & polish

  • ✓Admin panel: users, reports, audit log, no silent result edits
  • iMove-timing heuristics flag suspicious play — a first line of defense, not full statistical anti-cheat
  • ✓Privacy policy, terms, cookie consent, SEO meta, custom 404
  • ✓Hand-drawn SVG favicon & social image — no AI or stock art
